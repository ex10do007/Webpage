01 / The Problem

Enterprise work is fragmented by design

Knowledge, process, context and AI live in different places — documents and chats, approvals and tickets, projects and roles, disconnected copilots and pilots. Employees carry the integration burden, manually assembling the right information, tool and approval path for every task.

xAIOS moves the integration burden from the employee into the platform.

02 / Platform Thesis

One intelligence layer above existing systems

xAIOS does not replace systems of record — Microsoft 365, SAP and ERP, CRM and ServiceNow, enterprise data. It turns them into a coherent, governed work experience: assembling context, selecting routes, enforcing policy, coordinating skills and capturing receipts.
